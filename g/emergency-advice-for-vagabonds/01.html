5 Hitchhikers, Backpackers, and Rubbertramps:

Stuck in a town and can't get out? This is how

you get a free bus ticket, free night in a hotel,

free gas vouchers, and free food! (self.vagabond)

It sometimes happens to the best of us: We get stuck in a town or city and it's nearly impossible to get out.

Perhaps hitchhiking is illegal in the state you are in, and the cops are constantly kicking you off the side of the road.

Perhaps you got busted by a bull in the trainyard, and now the hotspot is impossible to access.

Perhaps nasty weather suddenly came, such as snow or thunderstorms, and you aren't prepared to stay another night outdoors.

2

Perhaps your van or bus ran out of gas and it's illegal to panhandle with your gas can.

Perhaps you decided that living on the road is not your lifestyle, and you just want to get back home as soon as possible!

Anything can happen on the road. And if you are on the road long enough, most of these things will eventually happen to you.

However, there are WAYS to escape so that you can get back on the road, or get back home.

FREE GREYHOUND BUS TICKETS:

Almost every town and city in the United States offers emergency tickets for travelers that are trying to get back home or get to a job.

However, it will require you going to the local police station, and requesting tickets from the secretary or an officer on duty. They will run your name for warrants, however they do not search your bags.

They will ask you if you have any drugs or weapons on you, but they do not actually search you or your bag.

They will usually issue you a ticket for the very next greyhound. Check your local greyhound bus schedule and be ready for it, because the cops will not refund your ticket or buy you another one.

(Sidenote: Local church's will also issue you bus call every single church on the google list. You may have to call half a dozen church's before you find the church that provides aid, but its worth all the calling.)

3

FREE HOTEL:

Most towns will provide ONE free night in a hotel.

The hotel booking is usually done by the local police station, or a local homeless outreach center.

Some towns only allow this if the weather is below freezing, but many towns will do it for whatever emergency situation you are in.

(Sidenote: If your room is non-smoking, do NOT stink up the room with smoke. The hotel will hold the cops liable, and they have the right to throw you in jail if you are unable to pay the smoking fine. I repeat, do NOT fucking smoke if the room is nonsmoking, it is NOT worth the consequences.)

FREE GAS VOUCHERS:

If you are a rubbertramper or van-dweller, and you're vehicle runs out of gas, many towns will give you a gas voucher that is usually enough for a half tank or full tank of gas. These vouchers are usually issued by a local church, homeless outreach center, or even the local police.
