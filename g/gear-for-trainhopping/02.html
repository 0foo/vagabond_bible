Camelbacks are a good idea, but the latest ones are too big and bulky with far too much "flash" or shit you don't need. The best way is to find a bladder with hose/mouthpiece that will fit in your bag. This also has the benefit of keeping the water cool by not being in direct sunlight. Somethign as simple as wear it by itself. Try to find an old used military surplus and make sure the bladder doesn't stink.
