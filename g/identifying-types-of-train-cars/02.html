Maurice W. Graham:

"Steam Train Maury" Graham (June 3, 1917 – November 18, 2006) was best known as five-time holder of

the title "King of the Hobos", and was later known as "Patriarch of the Hobos". Born to a broken home in Ohio,

he was shunted from father to mother to aunt to married siblings. In 1931, at the age of 14, Graham began

riding the rails as a hobo during the Great Depression. He settled in

late 1930s, and worked as a cement mason and founded a trade school for masons. During World War II, he

served in the military as a medical technician. In 1969 he returned to the hobo life for another eleven years,

finally retiring in 1980.
